About#

I am a Data Analyst at the University of Pittsburgh and a Biostatistician at VA Pittsburgh. My current direction is clinical AI, real-world evidence, biostatistics, EHR-based modeling, and AI-assisted research workflows.

I completed my Ph.D. at Carnegie Mellon University and my M.S. at Georgia Tech. My research background spans clinical analytics, sustainability analytics, environmental systems, autonomous sensing, and applied machine learning.

Current Direction#
