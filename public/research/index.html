Research Themes#

My research work is organized around a simple trajectory: early work in environmental and engineered systems trained me to analyze complex decision systems; my current work extends that systems-thinking background into healthcare, where clinical data, patient trajectories, resource utilization, and AI-enabled workflows form another complex decision environment.

Sustainability & System-Level Analytics#

This theme captures my cross-domain background in system-level analytics, life-cycle thinking, transition metrics, and data-driven environmental modeling. It supports my broader identity as an applied AI and analytics researcher, while remaining secondary to my current healthcare focus.

Representative work

  • A stock-based framework for monitoring fossil persistence and renewable expansion in global power systems
    Energy, Ecology and Environment, 2026. DOI · Code

  • Integrating machine learning into life cycle assessment: Review and future outlook
    PLOS Climate, 2025. DOI · Code

  • Impact of polymer molecular weight on the efficiency of temperature swing solvent extraction for desalination of concentrated brines
    Desalination, 2022. DOI

Plain-language focus

Many transition metrics focus on renewable growth, but renewable expansion does not automatically imply fossil displacement. My work in this area uses stock-based and life-cycle perspectives to separate growth, persistence, replacement, and system-level impact.
