## Workflows

This section is the future home for reusable research workflows: practical systems that help turn clinical or interdisciplinary research questions into structured analysis, writing, and review outputs.

These are planned public assets. They are intentionally listed as empty placeholders until the materials are ready.

### Planned Workflow Areas

| Workflow | Purpose | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Table One Workflow | Build reproducible baseline characteristic tables from analysis-ready datasets. | Planned |
| Statistical Analysis Plan Workflow | Translate a clinical question into outcomes, exposures, covariates, estimands, models, and sensitivity analyses. | Planned |
| Manuscript Review Workflow | Review drafts for structure, claims, methods clarity, limitations, and evidence boundaries. | Planned |
| Reviewer Response Workflow | Organize reviewer comments, response strategy, revision tracking, and evidence-backed replies. | Planned |
| Data Dictionary to Analysis Dataset | Convert REDCap/EHR/data dictionary structures into analysis-ready variable maps. | Planned |
| AI-Assisted Literature Review | Create reproducible literature screening, synthesis, and evidence mapping workflows. | Planned |

### Direction

The long-term goal is to build a research workflow lab around clinical AI, biostatistics, real-world evidence, and AI-assisted scientific production.
