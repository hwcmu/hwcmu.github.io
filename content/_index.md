**Understand the question. Find it in the data. Make the analysis hold up.**

I work with clinicians and researchers to understand the question behind the analysis, how it is represented in real-world data, and what it takes to answer it rigorously.

I am especially interested in making research workflows clearer and more reproducible, while exploring where AI can genuinely improve the way we think, analyze, check, and build—not as a substitute for statistical reasoning or domain understanding.

### What I'm Exploring

- **Clinical concepts in messy data** — turning real clinical questions into defensible cohorts, variables, and longitudinal outcomes
- **AI-enabled research workflows** — testing where AI can meaningfully augment research and analytical work
- **Structure beyond rows and columns** — using network, trajectory, and complex-systems thinking to uncover relationships conventional analyses may miss

My engineering and environmental-systems background still shapes how I work: understand the mechanism, respect the complexity, and question stories that look cleaner than the data.
