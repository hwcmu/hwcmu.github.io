## Research Themes

My research work is organized around a simple trajectory: early work in environmental and engineered systems trained me to analyze complex decision systems; my current work extends that systems-thinking background into healthcare, where clinical data, patient trajectories, resource utilization, and AI-enabled workflows form another complex decision environment.

### Clinical AI & Healthcare Analytics

This is my primary current direction. I focus on clinical prediction, EHR-based modeling, clinical NLP, multimodal healthcare data, emergency care utilization, and practical model evaluation.

**Representative work**

- **Machine learning-driven prediction of hospital admissions using gradient boosting and GPT-2**  
  *DIGITAL HEALTH*, 2025. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/20552076251331319)

- **Integrating multimodal clinical data to predict intravenous (IV) fluid utilization**  
  *PeerJ Computer Science*, 2025. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj-cs.3441) · [Code](https://github.com/hwcmu/IVF-prediction)

- **Machine Learning for Personalized Prediction of Electrocardiogram (EKG) Use in Emergency Care**  
  *Journal of Personalized Medicine*, 2025. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/jpm15080358) · [Code](https://github.com/hwcmu/EKG-prediction)

**Methods and skills demonstrated**

- Clinical prediction modeling
- Clinical NLP and multimodal EHR analytics
- Healthcare utilization modeling
- Model evaluation, calibration, and subgroup thinking
- Translating clinical questions into reproducible analytical workflows

### Sustainability & System-Level Analytics

This theme captures my cross-domain background in system-level analytics, life-cycle thinking, transition metrics, and data-driven environmental modeling. It supports my broader identity as an applied AI and analytics researcher, while remaining secondary to my current healthcare focus.

**Representative work**

- **A stock-based framework for monitoring fossil persistence and renewable expansion in global power systems**  
  *Energy, Ecology and Environment*, 2026. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40974-026-00425-8) · [Code](https://github.com/hwcmu/stock-based-power-transition-monitoring)

- **Integrating machine learning into life cycle assessment: Review and future outlook**  
  *PLOS Climate*, 2025. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pclm.0000732) · [Code](https://github.com/hwcmu/LCA-AI_review)

- **Impact of polymer molecular weight on the efficiency of temperature swing solvent extraction for desalination of concentrated brines**  
  *Desalination*, 2022. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.desal.2022.116104)

**Plain-language focus**

Many transition metrics focus on renewable growth, but renewable expansion does not automatically imply fossil displacement. My work in this area uses stock-based and life-cycle perspectives to separate growth, persistence, replacement, and system-level impact.
